Junior College & Millennia Institute

From the 2028 JAE, JC admission shifts from L1R5 to L1R4 — one fewer subject, freeing time for broader development. HMTL plays a key role for students who take it.

🔄
Big Change: L1R5 → L1R4 for JC (from 2028 JAE)

JC eligibility threshold changes from L1R5 ≤ 20 to L1R4 ≤ 16. Students with 5 G3 subjects can now access JC — previously only available to 6-G3 students. MI threshold of L1R4 ≤ 20 is unchanged.

🌟
Higher Mother Tongue (HMTL) — Key Advantage for JC

If your child takes Higher Chinese / Higher Malay / Higher Tamil, their HMTL grade can be used as L1 if it scores better (lower points) than English Language. This can meaningfully reduce the L1R4 total. Additionally, scoring A1–C6 in both EL and HMTL earns 2 bonus points (subject to cap).

🎓 JC L1R4 Components (2028)
  • L1 — English Language or HMTL (whichever is better)
  • R1 — Best subject from Humanities
  • R2 — Best subject from Maths or Science
  • R3 — Best from Humanities, Maths or Science
  • R4 — Any 1 best-scoring subject
  • Threshold: Gross L1R4 ≤ 16
📚 MI L1R4 Components (2028)
  • L1 — English Language or HMTL
  • R1 — Best from Humanities (updated 2028)
  • R2 — Best from Maths or Science (updated 2028)
  • R3 — Best from Humanities, Maths or Science
  • R4 — Any 1 best-scoring subject
  • Threshold: Gross L1R4 ≤ 20 (unchanged)

Polytechnic Pathways

Two routes into polytechnic — direct Year 1 entry (ELR2B2) and the Polytechnic Foundation Programme (ELMAB3). The two use different aggregates and different rules, so they have separate calculators.

🆕
Poly Year 1: New Common Benchmark from 2028

All students assessed on 4 G3 + 1 G2 instead of 5 G3. The weaker B subject is mapped from G3 to G2 scale. Net ELR2B2 target: ≤ 22 points (previously ≤ 26).

📐 How ELR2B2 Is Calculated
  • EL — English Language (G3 points used)
  • R1, R2 — Two relevant subjects for your course (G3)
  • B1 — Best remaining subject (G3 points)
  • B2 — 2nd best remaining (mapped to G2 scale)
  • Subtract CCA bonus for the net score
🗺️ G3 → G2 Grade Mapping
  • A1, A2, B3 at G3 → Grade 1 at G2
  • B4, C5, C6 at G3 → Grade 2 at G2
  • D7 at G3 → Grade 3 at G2
  • E8 at G3 → Grade 4 at G2
  • 9 (Fail) at G3 → Grade 5 at G2
Higher Mother Tongue and ELR2B2

HMTL can be counted as a Best (B) subject in the ELR2B2 aggregate. However, there is no additional bonus point awarded for the use of HMTL as part of the ELR2B2 computation.

Polytechnic Foundation Programme (PFP)

1-year foundation programme > Enters Poly Year 1. Uses the ELMAB3 aggregate — not ELR2B2.

.
🔓
PFP Expanded to G3 Students from 2028

Now open to G3 or mixed G2+G3 students, with G3 grades mapped to their G2 equivalents. Students enter one of 3 broad clusters rather than a specific diploma.

📌
All Four PFP Conditions Must Be Met

The aggregate alone is not enough — minimum subject grades apply as well:

  • ELMAB3 raw aggregate ≤ 12 points (English, Maths, Best 3 subjects), excluding CCA bonus points
  • English Language and Mathematics / Additional Mathematics: at least G2 Level Grade 3
  • One relevant subject: at least G2 Level Grade 3
  • The two other subjects making up the ELMAB3 aggregate: at least G2 Level Grade 4
🧮
Use the dedicated PFP calculator

Because PFP uses a different aggregate and has minimum grade requirements, it has its own tab in the Score Calculator — with G3 grades shown alongside their G2 equivalents in every dropdown, so G2 and G3 students can both key in their results easily.

ITE — Institute of Technical Education

All ITE courses upgrade to 3-Year Higher NITEC from AY2026, with expanded entry and a new guaranteed polytechnic progression route.

⬆️
All Courses → 3-Year Higher NITEC from AY2026

From AY2028, students scoring ELMAB3 ≤ 19 (at G2 level) can apply directly to Year 2 of Higher NITEC — skipping Year 1 and shortening their overall time at ITE.

📥 ITE Year 2 Direct Entry (from 2028)
  • Admission requirements set at G2 level
  • ELMAB3 ≤ 19 for Year 2 direct entry (there are other specific subject grade requirements - to refer to Coursefinder/ITE website in 2027
  • G3 subjects mapped to G2 for aggregate
  • Open to G3, G2, or mixed combinations
🌉 Accelerated Pathway at ITE
  • Students may be offered the accelerated pathway if they meet the academic requirements during Year 1 Semester 1 examinations. Students in this pathway will attain a Higher Nitec in a shorter duration of about two years.

Poly Progression Guarantee for Higher NITEC Graduates (from AY2027)

Higher NITEC graduates with GPA ≥ 3.5 are guaranteed a polytechnic diploma mapped to their ITE course. Top 10% (~1,000 graduates) guaranteed across all courses. The DPP pathway ceases from 2028. Minimum ITE→Poly GPA raised from 2.0 to 2.5 from 2027 poly intake.

Secondary 5

The option of a fifth year in secondary school.

📚
Option of a Fifth Year in Secondary School

The option of a fifth year in secondary school will continue to be available for eligible students to pace their learning and take subjects at a more demanding level to access more post-secondary pathways.

📌
Eligibility — meet either one of the following criteria

Students will be eligible for the fifth year if they meet either one of the criteria below.

1
3 or more G3 Passes, and do not qualify for JC / MI / Poly Year 1.
Students do not qualify for admission to Polytechnic Year 1 if they fail to meet the minimum entry requirements for any polytechnic diploma courses, or if their net ELR2B2 aggregate score is 23 points or higher (after accounting for CCA bonus points).
2
2 or fewer G3 Passes, and attain a gross ELMAB3 ≤ 21, ELB3 ≤ 14 or MAB3 ≤ 14, computed at G2.
Students will need at least Grade 5 at G2 for all subjects used in the computation of the aggregate.
✅
Offering all subjects at G3 in the fifth year

Only students who take mostly G2 with a gross ELMAB3 (at least Grade 5 for all subjects computed at G2) of 19 points or better can offer all their subjects at G3 in the fifth year.

💬
Not sure if you're eligible?

Students who do not offer sufficient G2 or G3 subjects to compute the eligibility criteria for the fifth year may consult their schools on their eligibility.

LASALLE College of the Arts & NAFA

From 2028, Singapore's two premier arts institutions adopt new SEC-based admission criteria. Creative talent still matters most — academic grades are just one part of the picture.

🎨 Arts Institutions · From AY2028

Pathways for Arts-Inclined Students

LASALLE and NAFA now use the new SEC qualification (applicable from 2028 intake onwards) as their academic benchmark. Portfolios, auditions, and interviews remain central to admission — fulfilling the academic criteria opens the door, but your creative work gets you in.

LASALLE / NAFA Year 1 — Direct Entry

📌
Two Conditions Must Both Be Met

Academic eligibility for LASALLE/NAFA Year 1 requires satisfying both of the following conditions simultaneously. Meeting only one is not sufficient.

1
Aggregate score of 21 points or better (i.e. ≤ 21) computed across 4 subjects — comprising 3 subjects at G3 and 1 subject at G2 — excluding English Language.
LASALLE assesses this against the raw aggregate (before CCA bonus points are deducted). NAFA assesses this against the net aggregate (after CCA bonus points are deducted) — so CCA points can help a student meet NAFA's cut-off but make no difference to LASALLE's. Subject-specific grade requirements may also apply — refer to each institution's website for details.
2
Required grades for English Language at G3 needs to be C6 or better.
⚠️
Important Notes

Eligibility does not guarantee placement. In addition to meeting the academic criteria, applicants must submit a portfolio and attend an admission test, audition, or interview depending on their chosen course. Requirements for LASALLE and NAFA may differ — always refer to the individual institution's website for the most up-to-date information.

NAFA Foundation Programme (NFP)

🌟
1-Year Art Foundation → NAFA Diploma

The NAFA Foundation Programme is a 1-year preparatory programme that leads directly into a NAFA diploma. It provides students time to build their creative foundation before entering diploma-level study. Upon successful completion, students are offered a place in their chosen diploma programme.

📐 NFP Academic Requirement
  • Singapore-Cambridge SEC — from 2028 intake
  • ELMAB3 ≤ 15 points (computed at G2 level)

G1 Students — Pathways & What to Expect

Students with at least 4 G1 subjects have a clear pathway into ITE from AY2028, with a new accelerated route for high performers. Every pathway forward is open to them.

G1 Students: Every Door Is Still Open

Taking subjects at G1 level does not close off a future. From AY2028, G1 students enter ITE Year 1 with a new possibility — high performers can bridge to Year 2 in just one semester, shortening their Higher NITEC journey significantly. Hard work in ITE opens the door to polytechnic and beyond.

📋

From AY2028, ITE Year 1 admission requirements for 3-Year Higher NITEC courses will be set at G1 ITE aggregate score, computed based on 4 G1 subjects. G3 and G2 grades are mapped all the way down to G1 equivalents using the combined grade mapping table.

💡 How to Read Your Score
  • For JC/MI: Lower is better. Eligibility uses the raw L1R4 (before bonus deductions); JC needs raw ≤ 16, MI needs raw ≤ 20. EL must be C6 or better, Mathematics must be D7 or better, and MTL must meet the minimum grade for its level.
  • For Poly Year 1: Lower is better. Eligibility uses the net ELR2B2 (after CCA bonus deductions); most courses need net ≤ 22. English, R1 and R2 must each be C6 or better (pass).
  • For PFP: Uses ELMAB3, not ELR2B2. Needs raw ELMAB3 ≤ 12 (CCA excluded) and all three minimum grade conditions.
  • For ITE: Lower is better. ELMAB3 ≤ 19 qualifies for Year 2 direct entry. G1 students use the separate G1 ITE Aggregate calculation.
  • For LASALLE/NAFA Year 1: Both conditions must be met — EL minimum grade (C6 or better, at G3) AND 4-subject agg (3G3 + 1G2, excl. EL) ≤ 21.
  • For NFP: Needs ELMAB3 ≤ 15, computed entirely at G2 level (including English, minimum Grade 3).
  • HMTL helps for JC/MI — it can serve as L1 and earn 2 bonus pts. It may count as a Best subject for Poly, but earns no bonus point there.
  • Always verify cut-offs on MOE CourseFinder and institution websites — requirements may differ.
